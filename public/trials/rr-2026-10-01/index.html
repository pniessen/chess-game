Model Chess Round-Robin

Seven AI models played every other model four times, two games with each colour. Stockfish, the open-source chess engine, judged every move afterwards. This page sets out who won, how well each model played, and what it cost in time and money.

Standings

Score is wins plus half the draws. Avg loss is how much worse a model's moves were than Stockfish's best, in centipawns: 100 is about one pawn, lower is better. Gemini 3.1 Pro and Opus 5.5 tied on points; Gemini ranks first on accuracy, while Opus won their own four games 2.5–1.5.

Strength against cost

Each dot is a model: its score against its average cost per game, on a log scale. Up and to the left is better value. Hover a dot for its numbers.

Head to head

Points the row model scored against the column model, out of four games. Darker means the row model did better.

Model by model

The style notes were written by Claude Opus 5.5 from the statistics only, with an instruction to claim nothing the numbers do not support. The bars show average loss per move in each phase of the game, on one scale for every model.
